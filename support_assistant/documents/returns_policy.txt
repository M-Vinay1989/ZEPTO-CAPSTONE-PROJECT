================================================================================
ZEPTO CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY: RETURNS & REPLACEMENTS
================================================================================

1. On-the-Spot Doorstep Inspection

- Customers are encouraged to inspect fresh produce, dairy, and perishable items upon delivery in the presence of the delivery rider.
- If any item is damaged, spoiled, missing, or expired, you may refuse the item at the doorstep for an instant item refund.

2. Return Window for Non-Perishable Goods

- Unopened packaged goods, personal care items, and household essentials can be returned within 48 hours of delivery via the Zepto app.
- Returns must include original packaging and invoice.

3. Pick-Up Process

- Once a return request is submitted, a Zepto delivery partner will be assigned to pick up the item within 2 to 4 hours.
- Refunds are initiated immediately after item pickup verification.
