ZEPTO CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY: PAYMENTS

1. Supported Payment Options
Zepto accepts multiple payment options including:
- Credit Cards and Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Amex)
- Unified Payments Interface (UPI via Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM)
- Net Banking across major banks
- E-Wallets (Zepto Wallet, Paytm Wallet, Mobikwik)
- Cash on Delivery (COD) for orders below ₹1,000 in designated pin codes.

2. Payment Failures & Pending Transactions
If money is debited from your bank account during a failed transaction attempt, the bank usually auto-reverses the amount within 24 to 48 hours. If the amount is not credited back within 48 hours, customers can raise a ticket with the transaction reference number (UTR) under App Support.

3. Duplicate Charges
In the event of a double debit or duplicate charge for a single order ID, Zepto's automated billing system detects the anomaly and initiates a full reversal of the duplicate charge within 2 business days.

4. Payment Confirmation
Order placement is confirmed only after receiving successful payment authorization from the payment gateway (except for COD orders).
