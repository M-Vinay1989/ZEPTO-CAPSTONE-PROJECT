================================================================================
ZEPTO CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY: ORDER TRACKING & DELIVERY PARTNERS
================================================================================

1. Live GPS Tracking

- Once your order is packed and dispatched from the local dark store, live GPS tracking becomes available in the Zepto mobile app.
- You can track your delivery partner's real-time location on the interactive map from dispatch until doorstep arrival.

2. Delivery Partner Contact

- The name, photo, and direct phone number of your assigned delivery partner are displayed on the order tracking screen once dispatched.
- You may call or send delivery instructions (e.g., 'Leave with security', 'Ring bell twice') directly via the in-app chat.

3. Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA) Updates

- ETA is calculated dynamically based on store packing status, distance, traffic, and weather conditions.
- If delivery is delayed beyond the initial ETA by more than 10 minutes, an automated notification is sent with an updated ETA and a complimentary delivery credit.
