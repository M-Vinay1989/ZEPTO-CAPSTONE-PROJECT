================================================================================
ZEPTO CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY: PRIVACY & DATA PROTECTION TERMS
================================================================================

1. Personal Information Collection

- Zepto collects customer names, delivery addresses, phone numbers, and transactional payment history strictly to process orders and improve services.
- Sensitive financial data (such as card CVV or UPI PIN) is never stored on Zepto servers and is processed via PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways.

2. Data Sharing Limitations

- Customer location data is shared with assigned delivery partners solely during active delivery fulfillment.
- Zepto does not sell or lease customer personal data to third-party advertisers.

3. Account Data Deletion Request

- Customers may request full account and data deletion through App Settings > Privacy > Request Account Deletion.
- Account deletion takes effect within 7 business days, after which non-transactional personal data is permanently purged.
